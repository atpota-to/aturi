# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small tool for a small network, and there is no version of a waypoint catalog that is worth being unpleasant over.

## What is expected

Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Take correction without treating it as an attack, and give it without making it one. Critique the code, not the person who wrote it. When you disagree, say what you think is wrong and why, and accept that the maintainer sometimes decides differently than you would.

Respect how people identify themselves. Use the names, handles, and pronouns they use. Getting it wrong once is nothing; being told and continuing is a choice.

## What is not

- Harassment, in the issue tracker or anywhere else the project reaches
- Slurs and demeaning language about who someone is, including sexuality, gender identity, race, disability, and religion
- Sexual attention or advances in project spaces
- Publishing someone's private information, including tying a handle or DID to a legal name they have not published themselves
- Sustained disruption: derailing threads, reopening settled decisions, brigading
- Volume as a weapon. Filing dozens of low-effort or machine-generated issues and PRs is a burden on one maintainer, and repeated after a warning it is treated as disruption rather than enthusiasm.

## Scope

The issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and any space where you are representing the project. Off-platform behavior toward people in this community can be grounds for action here when it makes participation unsafe.

## Enforcement

Report to [aturi@atpota.to](mailto:aturi@atpota.to) or DM [@aturi.to](https://bsky.app/profile/aturi.to). Reports stay private. If your report concerns the maintainer, say so and it will be handled with someone uninvolved.

Responses run from a private word, to a comment being removed, to a temporary block, to a permanent one. Which applies depends on severity and on whether it happened again after being raised. Judgement calls will be made, and they will not always be the call you would make.
