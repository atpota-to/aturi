# Popup, round 2: the copy-menu model, combined

The owner looked at the six models and chose **copy-menu** as the base, with three changes. Read `brief.md` first for the extension, the jobs, the materials and the system; this file is the spec for the combination. Start from `copy-menu/` (its `NOTES.md`, `post.html`, `nopage.html`, `style.css`, and the renders `post-light__view.png`, `post-closed-light__view.png`, `post-on-light__view.png`, `nopage-light__view.png`): keep its card with the two verbs (Copy ▾ opens a menu of the identifiers with their values beside the labels; Open on aturi.to hands off), the waypoints as shipped beneath it, and its no-data page's structure. The judges' notes on copy-menu to take on board: the AT URI and link were hand-cut in the menu ("did:plc:gq4fo3…/3mypi5zkyps2i") and the CID was dropped; a long PDS host or a did:web must survive the menu; the open menu covered the Recommended drawer.

## Change 1: records on the page are tabs on the card
The owner: "I want pages with multiple records to look and work better; maybe each record is a tab." So the card carries folder tabs along its top edge, in the site's folder-tab motif (the open tab is the card's own sheet, the filed ones a step below; see `../inspect/popup.css` `.popup-mode-tab` for the shipped numbers and `inspect-mockup-final-light__view.png` for the drawer-tab look): "This page" first, for the page's own record, then one tab per referenced record, labelled by the author's handle (`data.json` hits 3-7: kyefox.com, bjkeefe.bsky.social, dame.is, satyrs.eu, mitsuhiko.at). When the tabs overflow the card's width the strip scrolls sideways with a trailing fade, as the settings page's tab row does; decide what 40 references look like (a scrolling strip of 40 handles, or a strip plus a last tab that lists the rest) and say so in NOTES.md. Opening a tab changes the card's content: its title (e.g. "Post by kyefox.com"), kind and provenance ("In structured data"), its Copy ▾ and its Open on aturi.to. The duplicate hit (1 and 2, the same record) is one tab. The old "5 more records on this page" sentence goes. Think about whether a tab needs a mark (the author's avatar letter? the kind?) or just the handle, and about the page's own tab being distinguishable from the references.

## Change 2: keep "Seen lately"
The owner liked one-sheet's "Seen lately" (`one-sheet/nopage-light__view.png`: repos recently seen, with the PDS host and a page count; a row opens the repo on aturi.to). Keep it in some form: on the no-data page in place of copy-menu's "Recent" chips, and anywhere else it earns a place (the expanded search below is a natural one). Data to use: dame.is · pds.atpota.to · 12 pages; mitsuhiko.at · puffball.us-east.host.bsky.network · 4 pages; kyefox.com · puffball.us-east.host.bsky.network · 2 pages.

## Change 3: a search glyph in the header that expands into the aturi explore field
A magnifier in the top bar; pressing it expands the site's go-to field ("Go to a handle, DID or at:// URI", the same 36px field as aturi.to's header, moss focus edge) in place: the bar's middle becomes the field (brand stays; the field has a close) or the field drops in under the bar; decide and justify. Beneath the open field, "Seen lately" as suggestions, and typing shows a "Go to <typed> on aturi.to" row; Enter opens aturi.to/explore/<typed>. Esc or the close collapses it. The top bar must still hold the brand, the search glyph and settings; copy-menu's auto-redirect switch in the bar may stay if it fits at the small preset (360px) and does not crowd the glyphs, otherwise move it and say where (a filed row at the end of the sheet, or a strip only while it is on, as other models did).

## States to build (each a whole popup in `<body>`; render with `node render2.mjs <dir>`)
- `post.html`: the post page, "This page" tab open, Copy menu open with one row copied.
- `post-ref.html`: the same page with the "kyefox.com" tab open (menu closed), so the card shows a referenced record.
- `post-search.html`: the same page with the search expanded, "Seen lately" beneath it, nothing typed (or a few characters typed with the "Go to" row, your call).
- `nopage.html`: a page with no Atmosphere data: the notice, the search field (expanded by default here?), Seen lately, the app shortcuts.
- Optionally `post-many.html`: 40 references, to show the strip's behaviour.
Plus `style.css` and `NOTES.md` (the model; clicks per job; how the tabs scale; where auto-redirect, theme and settings live; what you changed from copy-menu and why; what you are unsure of). Use the fixture strings and `icons.json` marks. Stay on tokens; one hairline; the two radii; Figtree and Plex Mono; dark must hold.
