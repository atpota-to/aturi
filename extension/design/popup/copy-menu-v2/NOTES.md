# Copy menu, combined: record tabs, Seen lately, header search

## The model in a paragraph
The popup is one sheet. At the top sits one index card for the record the page is about, and along the card's top edge stand folder tabs, one per record the page carries: "This page" first, pinned, then one tab per referenced record labelled by its author's handle, in a run that scrolls sideways and fades at its ends, and at the strip's right end a **+N** tab that counts the records the strip is not showing and opens a filed list of all of them under the card. The open tab is the card's own sheet: opening another tab swaps the card's content (avatar, "Post by kyefox.com", kind and where on the page it was found, a two-line preview, Copy ▾, Open on aturi.to). The card carries exactly two verbs: **Copy ▾**, which unfolds a ledger of the record's six identifiers inside the card (AT URI, Link, DID, Handle, PDS, CID, each with its value beside the label, long ones wrapped at a slash or a dot), and **Open on aturi.to**, which hands the record to the site where everything else lives. Below the card the waypoint drawers follow as shipped. The top bar holds the brand, the global Auto-redirect switch, a search glyph and settings; the glyph pulls a drawer out from under the bar with the site's go-to field and "Seen lately" beneath it as suggestions, over a scrim that dims the page. On a page with no Atmosphere data the card holds the notice and the field, Seen lately follows as its own sheet, then the app shortcuts.

## Regions
- **Top bar** (sticky): brand mark top-left; at the right end, the global **Auto-redirect** switch (icon, label, small pill), the **search glyph** (pressed while its drawer is open; its tooltip says `Go to a handle, DID or at:// URI (/)`) and the settings button. Rendered at every preset (`check-presets.mjs`, `preset-*-bar.png`): the right-hand group is 223px at small (360), 229 at medium (380), 235 at large (420), 245 at xlarge (460), leaving 63px of free run between brand and controls at small and 131 at xlarge, so the switch stays in the bar with its label at every size and never crowds the magnifier.
- **Search drawer** (floating, from the bar's bottom edge; `post-search`, `post-search-typed`): the site's 36px go-to field at full width ("Go to a handle, DID or at:// URI", magnifier left, a close at its right end, moss edge and glow when focused), then "Seen lately" with the three repos (letter mark, handle, PDS host, page count; a row opens the repo on aturi.to/explore). Typing filters the rows and puts a "Go to `mitsu` on aturi.to · Enter" row on top; Enter opens aturi.to/explore/<typed>. The drawer covers the bar's hairline so bar and drawer read as one grown bar; it is the only thing that floats, so the only thing with a shadow. Under it a **scrim** (`--bg-primary` at 60%) dims the page from the bar's bottom edge down: the card is still there, visibly covered, and a click on the scrim closes the drawer exactly as Esc and the close do, with focus back on the glyph. Field and rows are one combobox: the input owns the listbox (`aria-controls`), rows are options the arrows move through (`aria-activedescendant`), Tab goes from the field to the close and on, never into the rows.
- **The card and its tabs** (`post`, `post-ref`, `post-refs`, `post-many`): the tabs use the shipped `.popup-mode-tab` construction and numbers (5px 12px padding on a 20px line, 32px filed, 35px open, the 4° wedges, the filed tone a step below the page) with the open tone set to the card's white, so the open tab runs into the card through its top border. "This page" is pinned outside the scrolling run and carries a small window glyph: filed, it still reads as the page and not as one more handle. The run (`.cm2-tabs-scroll`) holds the first twelve references in page order, rests on whole tabs (`scroll-snap-type: x proximity`, `scroll-padding-inline: 40px`, so a tab scrolled to lands clear of the fades), and fades over 40px at its end; scrolled, its first 6px (the gap) are fully clear and the previous tab's tail dissolves over the next 34px, so nothing torn butts against "This page"; at the end of the run only the start fades. The **+N tab** stands after the run in the same tab furniture: N is the number of references not fully in view (at most 8px under a fade), recomputed on load, scroll and resize, so it reads +4 on `post` and +39 on `post-many` and changes as the run moves; its tooltip and accessible name say "4 more records on this page · list them all". Every tab has a `title` ("Post by kyefox.com · in structured data"; the pinned one "The page's own record · Post by dame.is"). All gaps in the strip are the run's 5px; the glyph marks the pinned tab and the fade marks the run, so no second gap is needed. A handle longer than 150px ellipsises. The card: avatar letter, "Post by <handle>", `app.bsky.feed.post · on Bluesky` for the page's own record and `app.bsky.feed.post · in structured data` (the hit's `where` label, lowercased after the middot) for a reference, a two-line preview on references only, then Copy ▾ and Open on aturi.to for that record.
- **The records list** (`post-refs`): pressing +N unfolds a sheet directly under the card, "Records on this page", one shipped row per referenced record in page order: letter mark, handle, `Post · in structured data`. A row opens that record's tab and scrolls it into view; the open record's row carries the accent on its name and a tick at its end (`aria-current`). The sheet is a disclosure like the ledger: it stays open until +N is pressed again, so flipping through references from the list is one click each. Its tab furniture: the +N tab comes forward the hover step while the list is open (it is not the card's sheet, so it never takes the open tone).
- **The Copy ledger** (`post`): Copy ▾ unfolds six rows inside the card, below the verbs, full-bleed to the card's edges with a hairline between rows: AT URI, Link, DID, Handle, PDS, CID. Label column 50px, value in Plex Mono at `--fs-sm`, the rkey in the primary tone at 500 so the record's own name stands out of the path. It is a disclosure, not a menu (`aria-expanded` on Copy, a `<ul>` of buttons): it stays open after a pick and across tab switches, so copying the same identifier from several records is one click each, and it pushes the drawers down instead of covering them. Copied: the row keeps its name, takes the accent-soft family and gets a tick at the end of its last line; the Copy button above reads "✓ Copied" in the shipped `is-copied` tint; a visually hidden live region says "AT URI copied". Nothing moves. The state lasts 1.5s (the shipped button's timing), and switching record tabs clears it at once.
- **Waypoint drawers**: "Recommended for Posts" (accent tab), then the user's groups, rows as shipped.
- **Per-page band** (`post-on`): when auto-redirect is on, the card's bottom edge says "Auto-redirect is on · Pause for this page"; paused, the band goes amber with "Resume". "For this page" rather than "on this tab", since the card now has tabs of its own.
- **No-data page** (`nopage`): the same card with one tab, now a static label (no tablist, no `role="tab"`: a tab you cannot leave is noise to a screen reader) with its glyph in the tertiary tone, since the open folder is empty; the card holds "No Atmosphere data on this page." and the go-to field, focused on open (moss edge and glow) with a faint `/` keycap at its end at rest that goes while the field has focus. The bar's glyph here is a plain shortcut that focuses the field: not pressed, no `aria-expanded`, nothing to expand. Then a "Seen lately" sheet (drawer tab, the three repo rows with host and count, the count inside the link so the whole row opens the repo); then "Jump to an app" and the shortcut drawers at the shipped 40px, the same height as the Seen lately rows above them.

## The three jobs, in clicks
- **Jump**: 1 click on a row; the recommended drawer is the first thing under the card.
- **Copy**: 2 clicks, Copy then a row; a second identifier is 1 more. Keyboard: Tab to Copy, Enter unfolds the ledger with AT URI focused, Enter copies, so the URI is Tab · Enter · Enter; Tab (and Up/Down) move between rows, Esc folds the ledger and returns focus to Copy.
- **Hand off**: 1 click on Open on aturi.to.
- **A referenced record**: 1 click on its tab, then the same two verbs; a record beyond the run is +N then its row, 2 clicks. Keyboard: the strip is a tablist with a roving tabindex; Left/Right move, Home/End jump; the focused tab, not only the open one, scrolls fully into view (the snap padding keeps it clear of the fade). Mouse: a vertical wheel over the run moves it sideways; the trackpad and shift-wheel work as they do.
- **Look something up**: 1 click on the glyph (or `/` anywhere outside a field), type, Enter; a Seen lately row is 1 click once the drawer is open. Keys in the drawer: Down from the field highlights the first option (the Go row when something is typed, else the first Seen lately row), Up/Down move, Enter opens the highlighted row and with nothing highlighted goes to the typed text, Esc clears the highlight first and then closes the drawer with focus back on the glyph; a click on the scrim or the close does the same. On the no-data page the field is already there and focused: type, Enter.
- **Esc order** when both are open: the drawer first (it is the mode on top), then the ledger on the next press.

## Where the referenced records went
Into the card's tabs, and past what the strip shows, into the records list under the card. The duplicate hit (1 and 2, the same record found two ways) is one tab; hits 3-7 are five tabs. The "5 more records on this page" sentence is gone; +N says it in tab furniture and opens the list. Previews, engagement counts, raw JSON: on aturi.to; a reference keeps a two-line preview on its card because it is the only thing that tells two posts by the same author apart (dame.is appears as the page and as a reference here).

## Where auto-redirect, the pause, theme and settings live
- **Auto-redirect** (global): the top bar, beside the glyphs; it fits at every preset (see Top bar) and reads the same on every page.
- **Pause for this page** (per tab): the card's bottom band, only when auto-redirect is on.
- **Theme**: Settings → Appearance. **Settings**: the bar's right end.

## What changed from copy-menu and why
- **The floating menu became a ledger inside the card.** The judges noted the open menu covered the Recommended drawer and that the values were hand-cut with the CID dropped. Unfolded inside the card the ledger hides nothing, has the card's full width for its values (an AT URI's DID segment fits one line beside its label at every text preset: 38 characters in 276px at the medium one), takes the CID back, and stays open across tab switches. The cost is that the drawers move down ~260px while it is open; the first recommended row stays inside the first 600px.
- **Values wrap at joints, not with an ellipsis.** The markup carries a `<wbr>` after every slash and before every dot; a CID has no joints and wraps where it must. `overflow-wrap: anywhere` is the fallback for a did:web or a host longer than a line.
- **The hand-off line is gone**; the tabs and the +N tab answer the badge's count.
- **"Recent" chips became Seen lately rows**, on the no-data page and in the search drawer, with the PDS host and the page count from one-sheet.
- **The search is in the bar on every page**, not only the no-data one.
- "Pause on this tab" became "Pause for this page".

## Scale
- **40 references** (`post-many`): the run holds the first twelve, the +N tab counts the rest with what the run hides (+39 at rest on mitsuhiko.at), and the list under the card is the primary path, forty shipped rows in page order. Twelve is where a run stops being something to read as it slides by; past it the list is faster than the arrows. If the open record is beyond the twelve, the run holds it too.
- **Three to twelve references**: the run scrolls and +N counts what is out of view; the list is there for whoever prefers it.
- **One or two references**: the strip is "This page" and the handles, no fade, no +N, no list.
- **Profile page** (no rkey): "dame.is", `profile · on Bluesky`; the ledger loses nothing (AT URI `at://did:…`, Link `aturi.to/profile/dame.is`, DID, Handle, PDS, the profile record's CID).
- **Long handles and hosts**: tabs ellipsise at 150px; ledger values wrap at joints; the card title ellipsises; a Seen lately host ellipsises at its end once it passes the row (the fixture's longest fits to the pixel at medium).
- **Text presets**: the bar holds at all four (numbers under Top bar); the ledger's longest one-line segment still fits beside its label at small and large; +N is measured, so it is right at every width.
- **Seen lately in the drawer**: three rows today; once the extension keeps a longer log, cap it at five with a final "All recent repos on aturi.to" row, so the drawer never grows past the first 600px. The sheet on the no-data page can run longer.

## Trade-offs I accept
- The ledger moves the drawers down while open; a menu would have covered them instead.
- Six tabs do not fit in 356px: the run scrolls from the first page with references, and the +N tab takes ~45px of the strip to say so. A user who wants a specific reference reads handles as they slide by or opens the list.
- The reference tabs carry no mark (avatar letter or kind): the handle alone keeps the tabs narrow, and a letter would not tell the reference "dame.is" from "This page" any better than the handle does; the card's preview does that. If kinds mix on a page (a profile page referencing posts, lists and feeds), a 12px kind glyph before the handle in `--text-tertiary`, the way the pinned tab carries its window glyph, would earn its place, and the list rows already say the kind.
- The "Copied" word lives on the button, not at the row's end: the AT URI's DID segment fills its line to within 2px, so anything in flow at the row's end would re-wrap the value for the length of the state and the row would jump twice. The row's tint, its tick and the live region say which row; the button says what happened.
- The card's height changes by a preview's worth between the page's tab and a reference's.
- "Auto-redirect" in the bar is still a word to learn; the switch's tooltip and the settings page explain it.

## Revisions (after the two critiques)
- **The strip's count became a +N tab** in tab furniture outside the run, counting the references not fully in view (recomputed on scroll), and it opens a filed "Records on this page" list under the card (`post-refs`); the run is capped at twelve, rests on whole tabs with snap and 40px snap padding, and its start mask is fully clear for the gap so no fragment sits against "This page". The strip's gaps are all 5px. (Both critics, must-fix 1; C2 nice-to-haves on the gap, the snap padding, the wheel and the 12-tab fallback.)
- **A scrim under the search drawer**, a click on it closes the drawer, and the drawer now hangs from a positioned inner box inside a sticky wrapper (`.cm2-bar` > `.cm2-bar-inner`), so the full render keeps it under the bar. I kept the float over pushing the card down: a lookup leaves the page, and the popup is only 600px tall, so a pushing drawer would put the card below the fold while it is open. (C1 must-fix 2.)
- **The no-data glyph is a plain focus shortcut** (no `is-pressed`, `aria-expanded` or `aria-controls`); the field is autofocused and renders in its focus look; the lone tab is a static `<span>` with its glyph in the tertiary tone. (C1 must-fix 3, C2 must-fix 2, C2 nice-to-have on the glyph.)
- **The ledger is a disclosure**: `aria-expanded` + `aria-controls` on Copy, a `<ul role="list">` of `<li><button>` rows, Tab between rows, a visually hidden live region ("AT URI copied") instead of `aria-live` on the row. The copied row keeps its name; the state is the row's tint and a tick at the end of its last line (out of flow) plus the Copy button reading "Copied" in the shipped `is-copied` tint. Not at the row's end as both critics drew it, for the reason under Trade-offs: the AT URI would re-wrap. The pressed Copy edge is now `--border-strong`. (C1 must-fix 4, C2 nice-to-haves.)
- **The search field is a combobox** over a listbox of options with a "Seen lately" group; the key map is under "Look something up". (C1 must-fix 5.)
- **Provenance is lowercase after the middot** ("· in structured data"), on the card and in the list rows. (C2 must-fix 3.)
- **One row height on the no-data page**: the shortcut rows' 36px rule is gone, every row is the shipped 40px; the drawer's Seen lately rows are 40px too with the handle at the row's size, so Seen lately is one construction in both places. (C2 must-fix 4, C1 and C2 nice-to-haves.)
- **The close button's radius is `calc(var(--radius-control) - 4px)`**, the nested 4px of the letter marks and keycaps. (C2 must-fix 5.)
- **Tooltips**: every tab has a `title`; the glyph's says `(/)`; a faint `/` keycap sits in the no-data field at rest. The Seen lately count sits inside the row's link. NOTES now says what happens to the copied highlight on a tab switch, how long it lasts, the Esc order, the wheel mapping, that focus scrolls a tab into view, and the five-row cap for the drawer. (C1 nice-to-haves.)
- **Rendered the bar at the four presets** (`preset-*-bar.png`); the switch holds everywhere, so no icon-only fallback is needed. (C1 nice-to-have.)
- Not taken: a letter mark or kind glyph on reference tabs (reason under Trade-offs; the list rows carry both); `aria-haspopup` on the +N tab (it is a disclosure of a sheet, not a menu, so `aria-expanded` + `aria-controls` alone).

## What I am unsure of
- Whether the owner wanted the Copy menu to stay a floating menu; the inline ledger is my answer to "the open menu covered the Recommended drawer", and it is one `position: absolute` away from floating again if the covering is preferred to the push.
- Whether "Copied" on the button is enough of a word, or the row's end should carry it even at the cost of a re-wrap on the AT URI row (every other row has the room).
- Whether the records list should close after a pick (one click, then the sheet goes) rather than stay as a disclosure; it stays for the same reason the ledger does.
- Whether "This page" should say the handle instead ("dame.is") when the page's author is also a reference; "This page" avoids two identical tabs, which is why it stays.
- The 35 extra handles on `post-many` are invented; the first five are the fixture's. The scrim's tone (60% of `--bg-primary`) is a judgement; 50% reads thinner, 70% starts to hide the card.
- Whether the dissolving tail at the run's start (the faint "eu" on `post-many`) still reads as torn; it is now the fade and nothing else, clear for the gap's width.
