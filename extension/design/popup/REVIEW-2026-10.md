## 1. What the review found

**What already works.** The extension speaks the Field Notes dialect: paper page, white
cards behind one `--border-medium` hairline, two radii, moss as the only accent, Figtree
and IBM Plex Mono, tokens for colour and type, light and dark drawn from the same rules.
The popup's top bar is the best thing on either surface: its Waypoints/Inspect tabs mirror
`tabs.css` number for number (gap 5, pad 5/12, line 20, rise 3, 4° cut at ≥1.5dppx, 50%
hover mix, 62% dark filed mix) and read as the site's folder tabs. The Inspect tab's
"frequently seen" panel already uses the site's tiles-in-a-white-panel idiom, the group
manager's drag-and-drop is solid, and the ≤820px options nav has the right idea (baseline,
inset, trailing fade, scroll-into-view). No finding asks for a redesign.

**Where the weight sits.** Four clusters carry most of the 44 kept findings:

1. *The options nav* (6 findings, 2 high). At ≥821px it is still the retired green pill
   list; the site turned the folder on its side (account.css 34–101, SPEC §4c). At ≤820px
   the row is a loose mirror of tabs.css: 3px gap, fractional 19.6px line box, no cut, no
   bold-width reservation, hover tone that inverts in dark, 55% filed mix, -2px ring. It
   is five Tab stops with no arrow keys.
2. *The popup's list surfaces* (8 findings, 2 high). Group headings float as bare bold
   lines; the site's picker, which the popup list mirrors, stands each name on its box as a
   drawer tab. Tiles sit loose on the paper at four 83px columns that truncate names; the
   site boxes them and shows the names whole. Row copy buttons are thirty chain-link glyphs
   always on; the site uses the Copy glyph, revealed on hover. Both popup loading states
   open bare and the chrome pops in a frame later.
3. *Keyboard and state* (9 findings). Tablists on both surfaces without roving focus,
   SearchSelect with no accessible name and no key path through its list, a switch that is
   a pressed button with a square ring around a pill, moss focus rings in dark under 3:1,
   validation errors shown before the user has typed, and reduced motion honoured only by
   the popup's tabs.
4. *Number hygiene* (the rest). 105-character lines where the site caps at 68ch; 26–30px
   controls under the 32px floor with no touch variant; literal px where tokens exist
   (header, badge, breadcrumb, JSON, count); inner radii using the outer 12px; amber used
   for information tags; an inset divider; a 28px/400 segmented control beside the site's
   28px/500.

**Against the site.** Same product, same vocabulary, drawn one revision behind. The
differences that stay are the ones with a reason: the popup is 380px wide under a 600px
cap, so its rows, tabs (32/35 vs 38/41) and the new drawer tabs (26 vs 28) are one step
denser than the site's; the extension has no colour schemes, no in-app reduce-motion
toggle (so the OS media query is the hook), and honours text presets where the site's tabs
are fixed at 14px (so its nav column is 180px, not 160). Everything else below is the
site's own number or construction, mirrored by hand.
